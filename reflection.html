A personal reflection on the kind of online person I want to be.

When I’m annoyed, it’s easy to reply straight away. I want to get better at taking a breath first. A calmer message — or no message — is usually better than one I regret later.

I’m not going to get every interaction right. I can still own it, apologise, and make a better choice next time. There’s a real person on the other side of the screen.
