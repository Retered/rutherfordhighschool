02 / BEFORE YOU SEND

Five quick
checks.

No lecture. Just a few useful habits.

01

Pause

Would you still send it after a minute?

✳
02

Check the tone

Could it come across harsher than you mean?

✳
03

Ask first

Get permission before sharing a photo or screenshot.

✳
04

Don’t forward harm

Skip the share. Report it instead.

✳
05

Back someone up

Check in privately, and get an adult involved if needed.
