01 / WHAT CAN GO WRONG?

Online harm
is still harm.

A post can feel quick. Its effect might not be.

Repeated abusive messages, threats, sharing private information, or deliberately humiliating someone can cause real distress. The Harmful Digital Communications Act 2015 sets out ways to address serious harm caused through digital communications.

This page is a simple introduction, not legal advice. If something is happening to you or a friend, talk to a trusted adult and get support rather than handling it alone.

02 / SPOT THE PATTERN

Some things to watch for.

A

Targeted messages

Repeated insults, threats, or unwanted contact.

B

Sharing without consent

Passing around private details, photos, or screenshots.

C

Public humiliation

Using posts or group chats to shame or isolate someone.

✳

Not sure what to do? Save the messages, report them on the platform, and ask a trusted adult for help.
